# VERDE AUTO. Interview brief

Source: user interview, 24 October 2026. Working name and implementation choices below are authored decisions, open to revision. The user explicitly requested a plan and the start of construction in this session.

## User answers

1. Feeling: «человек должен почувствовать себя комфортно», «максимально анимированный, с хорошим дизайном», «чтобы его привлекло наше предложение».
2. References: landonorris.com is primary. Requested helmet hover image reveal adapted to cars, mixed premium type, brand marquee, moving lines, fan of cards, sculpted footer and full-screen burger menu. Terminal Industries is secondary, only for video effects.
3. Journey: supplied six-second car arrival video on entry, then left-side copy. Brands, cars with prices, selecting BMW and opening a car with a short description; a visually strong email invitation at the end. The rest of the sequence is delegated to design judgment.
4. Energy and emotion: comfort, attraction and spectacle across scroll, menu and buttons. «вау-момент ... везде». Authored pacing decision: movement throughout, with calmer information intervals so the main transition retains impact.
5. Signature seed: «когда наводишься на шлем, там появляется фотография ... когда мы сгенерируем фотографии для машин ... также». Authored extension: a road contour visually connects the collection and delivery journey; the selected car carries into the contact invitation and email draft.
6. Aesthetic family: expressive premium automotive editorial, large grotesk with elegant serif and drawn signature accents. Final palette explicitly supersedes the earlier blue idea: «зелёный, без синего, белый, зелёный ... тёмно-зелёный, чёрный».
7. World/chapters: user asks Scrollcraft to choose. Authored decision: distinct scenes with controlled transitions, not one continuous camera flight.
8. Assets: supplied MP4 only, fictional business; generated Mercedes and BMW photography requested. No existing name, logo, copy or real prices.

## Offer and visitor action

Car selection, purchase and delivery from China to Russia, including transport-only service. Primary action: «Обсудить автомобиль». This is a portfolio concept. Model specifications and prices are clearly identified as demo data, with no invented delivery statistics or testimonials. Email is a configurable placeholder; copying it is real, sending email is left to the visitor's mail application.

## Feeling curve, before scene planning

- Anticipation: the actual car enters the supplied footage and the left headline follows its arrival.
- Confidence: concise explanation of selection and delivery, with a calmer readable composition.
- Desire: full-scale car photography, brand filters and tactile image reveals.
- Wonder (peak): a route line draws across a dark landscape-like surface while the selected car's card becomes a delivery pass. This is the largest authored scroll sequence.
- Delight: overlapping photographs open into an animated fan.
- Resolve: a sculpted dark-green contact plate holds still, selected car remembered, a clear email action.

Peak / visitor sentence: «Это сайт, где выбранная машина становится пропуском в путешествие из Китая до твоего гаража».

Authored silence: product descriptions, prices and model details remain still while read. No empty pinned screens or mandatory intro waiting. Motion-off mode keeps all information accessible.

## Layer contract for supplied hero

The requested MP4 remains intact as the photographic plane; no artificial extraction or parallax of a baked-in car. Its existing camera/subject motion supplies physical depth. Independent planes: semantic text, restrained road-line foreground and UI. On scroll the video frame subtly scales while copy moves separately and an off-white transition surface enters. Mobile receives its own taller text placement and a lower, car-preserving video crop. This deliberately follows the supplied-video instruction over the skill's default multi-cutout hero.

## Catalogue expansion, user steering during implementation

«в каждом отсеке, например BMW ... минимум по 7-8 моделей и каждому сделать описание ... не только одну фотографию, а несколько ... с других ракурсов». Scope updated to eight BMW, eight Mercedes-Benz and eight Porsche models, with three generated photographic angles per model (front three-quarter, side profile, rear three-quarter), descriptions, specifications and illustrative prices. Image sheets are generated per model to maintain consistency, then exported as individual view files. Availability is not represented as verified real inventory.
